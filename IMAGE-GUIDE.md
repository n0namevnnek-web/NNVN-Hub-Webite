# NNVN Hub Image Guide

## Doi anh giao dien

1. Tao thu muc `assets/images/` trong cung cap voi `index.html`.
2. Dat anh vao do, vi du:
   - `assets/images/hero-banner.jpg`
   - `assets/images/blox-fruits-card.jpg`
   - `assets/images/anime-adventures-card.jpg`
3. Mo [app.js](C:/Users/ADMIN/Documents/Codex/2026-08-23/gi/app.js) va doi truong `image` trong tung item:

```js
image: "assets/images/blox-fruits-card.jpg"
```

4. Neu muon doi anh nen lon o dau trang, ban co the them CSS nen trong [styles.css](C:/Users/ADMIN/Documents/Codex/2026-08-23/gi/styles.css) o phan `.anime-hero`.

## Size nen dung

- Anh card script: `1280 x 800`
- Anh banner / hero: `1600 x 900`
- Dinh dang: `.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`

## Lay anh o dau

Ban nen dung anh co quyen su dung ro rang hoac anh tu lam. Neu muon chat anime:

- Tu ve / tu chup / tu thiet ke
- Dung anh duoc cap phep tu kho stock
- Tao anh goc rieng theo phong cach anime, tranh lay nhan vat co ban quyen

## Goi y giao dien anime

- Mau nen: xanh dem, hong nhat, xanh ngoc
- Them glow mem va hat sang nho
- Card nen co anh minh hoa nhan vat, thanh pho neon, hoac sky scene

## Ghi chu

Ban dang xem ban demo tinh. Neu muon upload anh that len mang va dung o moi may, buoc tiep theo la dua web len GitHub Pages hoac Cloudflare Pages.
